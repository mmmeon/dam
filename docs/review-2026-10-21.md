Behaviour that changed on purpose, and where it is written down:

- Moonlight: RTSP and bare `PING` only from the address that launched; one ANNOUNCE a launch;
  Encrypt Video "Always" asks for encryption and refuses plain video; connections capped (64, 16
  an address) with 10 s to ask (docs/moonlight.md).
- RustDesk: within 0.5 s of an open any desktop line counts; after, only video capture starting
  (docs/remote-sessions.md).
- OpenDisplay: a cable plugged in mid-stream is dialed beside the Wi-Fi link and takes over once
  connected; a session's setup failing waits 5 s before dialing again; a session whose display
  goes ends.
- Browser stream: a viewer stalled for 10 s is let go, and the page starts again when its tab
  comes to the front.
- An added app's changed detection waits for its session to end.
- Unchecking the last refresh rate keeps it, rather than saving 60 Hz.

## Your call

Each changes intended behaviour; none is fixed.

- **Web pages can start sessions.** `dam://session/start?…&blackout=yes` from any page, after the
  browser asks once, makes a display and blacks the screens out (`AppDelegate.handleGetURL`,
  `ControlRequest.request(from:)`): ask before acting, or leave `blackout` out of URLs.
- **Shortcuts with only ⇧ or ⌥.** Recording ⇧A takes every capital A typed anywhere
  (`KeyRecorderView`): require ⌃ or ⌘.
- **OpenDisplay receivers can pose as one turned on.** A host that advertises a receiver's ID (it
  is in that receiver's Bonjour record) may be dialed and get the screen; OpenDisplay has no
  authentication (`OpenDisplaySender.found`, `session(_:saidID:device:)`). Narrow it: when two
  names claim one ID, dial the one known for it.
- **An OpenDisplay display removed from the menu comes back** about 5 s later, as the receiver is
  still on (`OpenDisplaySender.displayGone`): turn the receiver off when the removal was the user's.
- **Remote sessions aren't told when their display goes.** Only OpenDisplay hears
  `VideoManager.onStreamingDisplayGone`; a session keeps the ID for blackout and removal.
- **OpenDisplay's display is offered to Moonlight and the browser** (`AppDelegate.rebuild()`). Left
  as it is for now.

## Limits the fixes kept

- RustDesk: past the window, a line from a terminal still running drops a desktop still logging in
  (`SessionLogLines.RustDeskConnections`).
- Moonlight: the RTSP check assumes a client's HTTPS and RTSP come from one address (NAT, VPN,
  IPv6); Encrypt Video "Off the Local Network" stays opportunistic, as intended; control messages
  can be replayed (the GCM sequence isn't checked to rise, `MoonlightControlCrypto.open`).
- Touch Bar: DAM can't see the system's close box, so a closed resolution bar stays known and is
  refreshed unseen (`ControlStripPresenter.closeResolutionBar`).
- Arrange window: `mirror(_:onto:)` uses the map's layout, which can be stale; drops and the main
  display drag read a fresh one.
- Untested by unit tests: ControlSocket closing descriptors in the cancel handlers, and an
  OpenDisplay cable taking over starting the session's timers.

## Not confirmed

Raised by the review, not shown to happen:

- Moonlight: a fast re-ANNOUNCE or resume may fail to bind its UDP or ENet ports while the old
  ones are still let go; an unauthenticated `/unpair` or `getservercert` can reset a pairing in
  progress (a nuisance only: the PIN still guards it).
- Streaming displays: the termination handler matches by slot index, so a late one could forget a
  newer display in the slot (match the display object instead); a display placed while it ended is
  still handed to the caller; a failed add's completion might run twice if a completion adds
  again; the main display's hand-back isn't waited for; Control Center's tile route might toggle a
  device row.
- Anchors: the termination handler falls back to the device's name, which could forget a newer
  anchor of the same name; `airPlayScreenNameCache` and `ioKitNameCache` are never cleared, and
  display IDs come back; `waitForBootstrapDisplay` goes on polling after the stand-in is let go.
- Arrange window: Esc during a drag may leave it thinking a drag is on.
- OpenDisplay: a capture error from a subscription cancelled while it started could end a live
  session.
