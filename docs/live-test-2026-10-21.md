## Summary

Four problems came up. None has a log yet, so the causes below are leads from reading the code,
not findings. DAM's debug lines aren't kept: record them with
`/usr/bin/log stream --level debug --predicate 'subsystem == "mmmeon.dam"'` started before
trying again.

## 1. Browser streams drop often

Possibly older than the review; not known.

The review changed what a viewer that falls behind does (`WebStreamViewer.take` in
`DAM/WebStream.swift`, from `fix/media-webstream`):

- Before, it skipped to the next keyframe, which the session asked for at most every 250 ms.
- Now it skips until what it holds has drained to half of `maxInFlight`, keyframes for it come at
  most every 2 s (the catch-up throttle), and after `patience` (10 s) behind it is closed. The
  page then reconnects only when its tab comes back to the front (`a781952`).

On a link that is slow in bursts that means longer freezes, and closed streams. In the log:
`viewer behind by … bytes` and `viewer behind for 10.0 s; letting it go`. If that is it, put the
quick recovery back for a viewer that drains soon, and keep the throttle and the cut-off for one
that is stalled (a tab in the background).

Other changes in the same area, less likely: frames that would run ahead of the clock are no
longer encoded (`FrameGrid.snap`); late sound is resynced by dropping samples
(`alignment(gap:)`, `MoonlightAudioEncoder.encode(_:skipping:)`), and a browser shows no video
past the end of its sound; a connection must send its request within 10 s of being accepted
(`requestTimeout`).

## 2. The pointer is stretched or squashed after turning the iPad

Likely new: rotation worked when the sender was first tried (docs/opendisplay-sender.md).

The iPad draws the pointer picture at a size DAM gives as a fraction of the display
(`OpenDisplayReply.cursorImage`, `nw`/`nh` = picture size ÷ `CGDisplayBounds`), sent only when
the picture changes (`OpenDisplayCursor.update`). A turn resizes the display in place and
`startMedia()` makes a new `OpenDisplayCursor`, which sends the picture again. If that happens
while the display still has its old bounds, or the iPad keeps the old fractions, the picture
is drawn at the old shape.

What changed under it in the review (`fix/display-model`): `resizeStreamingDisplay` sets the
display's `start` only once the switch succeeds; `switchStreamingDisplay` skips the commit when
nothing would change and completes at once; completions now run before `finishStreamingChange`;
the kept layout drops the display's mode before the switch. Check in the log that the resize's
`CompleteDisplayConfiguration` comes before the session's `startMedia`, and what `nw`/`nh` the
picture went with.

## 3. OpenDisplay's display can be streamed by Moonlight and the browser

Not from the review; left as it is for now. `AppDelegate.rebuild()` offers Moonlight (and so the
browser stream) every streaming display that isn't a stand-in, OpenDisplay's and remote
sessions' included. If unwanted: offer only displays made for Moonlight or the browser.

## 4. Connecting the TV made everything mirror one display

Not known whether older. With OpenDisplay, Moonlight and an external display up, connecting the
TV left all of them mirroring the same display.

Two leads:

- macOS: WindowServer applies a layout it saved for the new set of displays, and has saved ones
  here that mirror (docs/slow-display-removal.md). DAM puts the layout back only within a few
  seconds of its own streaming display changes (`streamingSettle`), and around an anchor's setup
  (`virtualAnchorArrangements`), not when a display it didn't make joins.
- The review's anchor changes (`fix/display-model`): `keepUserMirror` folds mirror changes into
  every anchor's kept layout; restores leave out each anchor's `anchorSetSlaves`; releasing an
  anchor points the displays set to mirror it at the iPad; `applyMirror` stops for an anchor
  released or renamed meanwhile (`currentName(of:anchorID:)`); commits that change nothing are
  cancelled. If the TV gets an anchor, look in the log for `step2`/`step3` and `restore:` lines,
  or their absence.

Worth knowing too: whether the TV was connected from DAM's menu or from Control Center.
